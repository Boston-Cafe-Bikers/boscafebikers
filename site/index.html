Your first ride

Boston Café Bikers is a casual cycling group built around getting more people comfortable cycling around the city. We meet up somewhere reachable by T or bike, roll out together, and finish at a café.

There's no membership, no dues, and no drop. Just show up, say hi, and order something from a local café at the end.

  • Most rides are Saturday or Sunday mornings, 10 or 11 am, 3-5 flat-ish miles.
  • No-drop, always. Nobody gets left behind.
  • All levels welcome. We keep a conversational pace.
  • BlueBikes-friendly. Rides start at Bluebikes stations.
  • Bring a lock, a water bottle, and enough cash or card for a coffee.
  • Helmets encouraged, lights required if we're out near sunset.

Ride announcements go out on Partiful. Photos and route teasers go on Instagram.
